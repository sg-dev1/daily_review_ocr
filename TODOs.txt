Objective
  - Improve text recognition (on Android) - uses Google's ML Kit (see modules/ocr-module/android)
  - Can ignore the ios version
Observations
  - Current interface just returns the whole string
  - Full stops at the end of a text are sometimes not regocnized (maybe resolution of image is too small)
  - It is jumped between text blocks (do not know how the sorting works, if there is a sorting at all)
  - Confusion between full stops and commas
  - Umlauts (ä, ö, ü) not regocnized properly (sometimes just removed, sometimes replaced with accent, e.g. á)
  - Newlines are set as they are regocnized in the text
  - Capital letter in the middle of a sentence
  - Buchstaben in Wort ausgelassen oder neue hinzugefügt (eine Rechtschreibprüfung mit ausbessern auf das wahrscheinlichste korrekte Wort sollte da helfen)
  - Orientation of image is sometimes wrong (e.g. flipped by 90/ 180 degrees)
  - Issues with distorted images in books
  - If there are bad lighting conditions (leading to unsharp images) the results are very bad,
    with good lighting you get quite accurate results (the rotation of the images seems not to matter, however it is a bit anoying when it is rotated)
